# Vocabulary expansion sources and review status

The practice pool has a 7,000-word core for every supported language, combining existing topic vocabulary with 6,156 English-seeded entries: the original 4,240 entries plus 1,916 additional entries. The generated entries are grouped into 1,500 core, 1,500 everyday, 1,500 intermediate, and 1,656 advanced words. The core course is 28 levels of 250 words, with ten 25-word stages per level. Topic-only entries are included once in the general course even when a word also appears in a focused topic. Italian-only source additions append after the established Italian sequence so existing stage positions do not shift.

English headwords are selected from the FrequencyWords English list and filtered against English WordNet lemmas from Open Multilingual WordNet. Neither source supplied the translations in this expansion.

Italian, French, Spanish, and Western Armenian translations were generated for this project. **Every generated translation is marked `pending-review`; none should be treated as verified.** Western Armenian additionally requires Nayiri/native verification.

Nayiri was used as a reference for the requested Western Armenian variety and traditional orthography. Nayiri's open lexicon release is described as Western Armenian data, but its available dataset does not provide English glosses. Individual dictionary lookups were restricted in this environment, so entry-by-entry verification could not be completed. Keep the review marker until a qualified reviewer checks the translations against Nayiri and/or a Western Armenian speaker.

- Nayiri Armenian Lexicon: https://nayiri.com/

- Strommen Inc., “1000 Most Common Italian Words”: https://strommeninc.com/1000-most-common-italian-words-frequency-vocabulary-using-the-80-20-principle/
- User-provided *Italian Study Guide* PDF (`attached_assets/Italian-Study-Guide_1792986426274.pdf`), vocabulary and verb-headword tables on pages 2–18.

The Strommen list is available as an Italian-only frequency topic. Its 1,000 source rows contain a few repeated pairs, which are collapsed in practice, and context-dependent English glosses. The study guide is available as a separate Italian-only topic with 886 distinct source pairs from 38 vocabulary and verb sections; exact duplicate pairs are collapsed and repeated English glosses are clarified by sense. Both topics remain marked `pending-review`. Clear typographical or obvious gloss errors were corrected during import, but that does not constitute a full language review. The guide's conjugation tables are not duplicated as separate vocabulary cards; verb headwords are included. The guide adds 518 new unique headwords to Italian general practice, bringing that pool to 7,664; French, Spanish, and Armenian remain at 7,000 each.
